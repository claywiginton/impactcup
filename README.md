# Impact Cup 2026

GitHub Pages build for the Impact Cup 2026 tournament.

## Formats
- Match 1 — Captain's Choice: 5 points
- Match 2 — Best Ball / Four-Ball Match Play: 5 points
- Match 3 — Singles: 10 points
- Final — 10v10 Alternate Shot: 4 points

## Match 2 handicap rule
Each player plays his own ball. The better net ball of the two partners is the side's score for the hole.

For Four-Ball Match Play:
- Determine each player's unrounded Back-9 Course Handicap.
- The lowest player in the foursome plays from zero.
- The other three players receive 90% of the difference from that lowest unrounded Course Handicap.
- Round the resulting Playing Handicap to a whole number.
- Strokes are allocated using the Back-9 stroke-index order.
