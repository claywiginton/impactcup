IMPACT CUP 2026 - HANDICAP ENGINE AUDIT

Authoritative rules used
- 9-hole Course Handicap: halve Handicap Index, round to nearest tenth, then apply 9-hole Slope/Rating/Par.
- Match 1, 2-player scramble: 35% lower unrounded CH + 15% higher unrounded CH; combine; round team Playing Handicap; lower team plays scratch in head-to-head match.
- Match 2, Four-Ball Match Play: lowest unrounded CH in foursome plays scratch; each other player gets 90% of unrounded difference; round each Playing Handicap.
- Match 3, Singles Match Play: 100%; each player's Playing Handicap is the rounded Course Handicap; lower PH plays scratch; higher receives full difference.
- Six-hole Singles allocation is an Impact Cup committee rule: allocate the full Front-9 match-play difference by Front-9 stroke index, then use only strokes landing on Holes 1-6.

Corrections made in this audit
1. Match 1: team Playing Handicaps are rounded separately after 35%/15% is combined, then compared.
2. Singles: each player's 100% Playing Handicap is rounded first, then the difference is taken. The prior build rounded the unrounded CH difference directly.
3. Admin and Presentation Mode now use the same centralized handicap calculation functions, eliminating duplicate calculation paths.
4. Four-Ball retained the correct USGA sequence but was moved into the same centralized engine.
