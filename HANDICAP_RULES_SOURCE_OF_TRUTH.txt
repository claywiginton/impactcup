IMPACT CUP 2026 - HANDICAP RULES SOURCE OF TRUTH
Version: 2026-10-15 USGA/WHS Source of Truth v1

1. COURSE INPUTS
- Tee: No. 2 men.
- 18-hole published rating/slope: 71.6 / 143.
- Tournament 9-hole inputs: Front 35.8 / 137 / Par 36; Back 35.8 / 149 / Par 36.
- Published men's stroke-index row used by the tournament:
  H1 13, H2 5, H3 11, H4 7, H5 15, H6 1, H7 3, H8 17, H9 9,
  H10 8, H11 12, H12 6, H13 18, H14 16, H15 2, H16 10, H17 14, H18 4.
- Front allocation order: 6, 7, 2, 4, 9, 3, 1, 5, 8.
- Back allocation order: 15, 18, 12, 10, 16, 11, 17, 14, 13.
- If the official tournament-day scorecard differs, the official scorecard controls and the app must be updated before play.

2. 9-HOLE COURSE HANDICAP - USGA RULE 6.1b
Course Handicap = (Handicap Index / 2, rounded to nearest tenth) x (9-hole Slope / 113)
                  + (9-hole Course Rating - 9-hole par).
Retain the full calculated Course Handicap for Playing Handicap calculations.
For plus Handicap Indexes, preserve the plus sign through the calculation. In code they are stored as negative values.

3. MATCH 1 - 2-PLAYER SCRAMBLE / CAPTAIN'S CHOICE
Official USGA allowance: 35% of the lower unrounded Course Handicap + 15% of the higher unrounded Course Handicap.
Impact Cup head-to-head match-play implementation:
- Calculate each side's unrounded 35%/15% team value.
- Lower team value plays from scratch.
- Subtract the two UNROUNDED team values.
- Round the absolute difference ONCE to the nearest whole stroke.
- Allocate strokes in ascending order of the published Front-9 stroke indexes.
Reason: USGA guidance says handicap allowances should be applied to unrounded values and, in match-play formats where the lowest side plays from scratch, to the unrounded difference. USGA does not publish a separate worked 2v2 scramble-match-play example, so this final relative-stroke step is a stated Term of the Competition.

4. MATCH 2 - FOUR-BALL MATCH PLAY
Official USGA allowance: 90%.
- Calculate all four players' unrounded Back-9 Course Handicaps.
- Lowest unrounded Course Handicap plays from scratch.
- For each other player: (player unrounded CH - lowest unrounded CH) x 90%.
- Round each resulting Playing Handicap to the nearest whole number.
- Allocate each player's strokes in ascending order of the published Back-9 stroke indexes.

5. MATCH 3 - INDIVIDUAL MATCH PLAY
Official USGA allowance: 100%.
- Calculate each player's unrounded Front-9 Course Handicap.
- Lower player plays from scratch.
- Calculate the difference between the two UNROUNDED Course Handicaps.
- Apply 100% and round the result ONCE to the nearest whole stroke.
Impact Cup 6-hole rule:
- The official 9-hole difference is allocated using the full Front-9 stroke-index order.
- Only strokes landing on Holes 1-6 are used.
- No 6/9 proration or compressed six-hole ranking is used.

6. STROKE ALLOCATION
USGA Appendix E: use the published stroke-index allocation. For nine-hole play on an 18-hole course, strokes are taken in ascending order from the published 18-hole allocation.
If more than nine strokes are due on a nine-hole side, every hole receives one before the sequence repeats for additional strokes.

7. PLUS HANDICAPS
Plus handicaps stay plus throughout the calculation. Handicap allowances move both plus and non-plus values toward zero. In match play, the lowest relative value is set to scratch and the opponent receives the resulting difference.

8. FINALE - 10v10 ALTERNATE SHOT
Impact Cup Committee Rule: SCRATCH.
USGA/WHS publishes a Foursomes allowance for two partners sharing one ball, but not for a 10-player side sharing one ball. No handicap strokes are applied to the finale.
